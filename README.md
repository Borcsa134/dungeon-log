Do you want to explore World of Warcraft and log your journey?

Then this is the addon for you!

# Features

*   Dungeon journal that keeps on growing as you progress throughout the game
*   Everything is hidden until you discover that specific dungeon/boss
*   Only rely on your exploration, no predefined loot tables for bosses

# How it works

*   At first, there is no dungeon that you discovered
*   When you enter a dungeon the first time, that dungeon entry opens
*   You can only see how many boss encounters are there and nothing more
*   After killing the respective bosses, you unlock the boss entry
*   You can only see the loot that was dropped when you killed the boss

## Currently supported dungeon/raid entries

*   Original vanilla dungeons
*   4 new forever dungeons (RoL, HoT, ES:W, CoD)
*   Onyxia raid
